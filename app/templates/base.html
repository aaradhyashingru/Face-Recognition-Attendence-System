Operator & HR Reference Guide

Essential daily workflows, biometric camera rules, and troubleshooting cheatsheet

Follow this simple 4-step workflow each working day to keep attendance effortless and error-free:
1
Open Kiosk Mode
Click Kiosk Mode in the navbar or launchpad. Press F to enter full-screen on your entrance terminal or tablet.
2
Staff Face Scan
Staff stand 1-2 feet from camera. System automatically recognizes face within 400ms and announces welcome chime.
3
Monitor Dashboard
Check Dashboard for live headcount, late arrivals outside grace period, and today's active attendance rate.
4
Handle Exceptions
Use Manual Punch for employees on outdoor duty or camera glitches. Review pending leave requests under Workforce.
The camera viewfinder uses color-coded laser HUD indicators so you always know what the system is doing:
GREEN RETICLE (Success)
Face verified against enrolled vectors. Check-In or Check-Out recorded with timestamp and confidence score. Chime rings.
AMBER RETICLE (Cooldown)
Employee already punched within the cooldown window (prevents duplicate accidental punches when standing near camera).
RED RETICLE (Unrecognized)
Person not registered in the system yet. Ask them to see an admin for 1-minute face enrollment.
FLASHING RED (Anti-Spoof Alert)
MiniFASNet detected a printed photo or phone screen reflection. System rejected the spoof attempt.
Enrolling a new employee takes under 30 seconds using deep metric SFace embeddings:
1
Enter Details
Navigate to Workforce & HR > Users. Click Enroll New User. Fill in employee code, full name, department, and role.
2
Set Grace Period
Optionally configure a Manual Grace Time (e.g. 20 minutes) if this employee has an authorized arrival buffer.
3
Capture 5 Frames
Position the employee looking directly at camera. Click Capture & Enroll. The system automatically records 5 burst frames.
Forgot to scan? Camera rebooted? Handle attendance corrections cleanly without touching the database:
CHECK-IN ONLY:
Records a single IN punch at any custom date & time with optional status (ON TIME, LATE, or EXCUSED).
CHECK-OUT ONLY:
Records a single OUT punch at the employee's departure time.
FULL SHIFT (Both IN & OUT):
Creates both Check-In and Check-Out in 1 click (defaults to 8 hours apart). Perfect for full regularized days.
